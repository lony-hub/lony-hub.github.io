Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect only what the site needs to work.

WhenWhat
You sign in with GoogleName, email address, profile photo and the time you last signed in. We never see your Google password.
You use your profileThe display name and avatar image you choose.
You pick a projectWhich projects you selected and whether you marked them completed.
You write a reviewYour review text, display name, avatar, optional link and up to three images. Reviews are shown to other students only after you mark the project completed.
You payThe project or pack you paid for, the amount, your 12 digit UTR (UPI transaction reference), your account ID and email, the time, and the verification status.
You browseYour browser sends standard technical data (such as IP address and device type) to our hosting provider and to Google services we use. We do not build advertising profiles from it.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account working.
  • To verify payments and unlock the projects you paid for.
  • To show your selected projects, progress and reviews.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent misuse, such as reused payment references.
  • To remember your choices (theme, guide language) if you allow it.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Where it is stored

Account, profile, project, review and payment records are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) under the LONY project. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Small preference values can also be stored on your own device (see section 4).

4. Cookies and device storage

When you first visit, you can choose Accept All, Reject Optional Cookies or Customize Preferences. You can change your choice at any time with the button below or the “Cookie Preferences” link in the page footer.

CategoryPurposeDefault
NecessaryKeeps you signed in (Firebase sign-in session), remembers your payment and unlock status, and stores your cookie choice.Always on
PreferencesRemembers your theme, guide language, phone or laptop choice and contact email on this device.Off until you allow it
AnalyticsNot used at the moment. If we add usage analytics in future, it will stay off unless you turn it on.Off

If you turn Preferences off, the saved values are removed from your device. Optional scripts are never loaded before you give permission.

5. Payments

Payments are made by UPI in your own payment app. LONY does not receive or store your card details, bank login or UPI PIN. After paying, you enter the UTR number. We keep that number, the amount and the project name so an administrator can check it against our bank records and unlock your project. If a payment cannot be verified, it is marked rejected and you can see this on the payment status page.

6. Ask AI feature

Inside project guides you can prepare a question for ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude AI (Anthropic) or Gemini (Google). The question is built on your device from the project details and your doubt. It is not sent to LONY. When you choose a provider, we copy the question and open that provider's own website in a new tab; what happens next is governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy. You may need to sign in there. LONY does not provide AI subscriptions, and never asks for or stores your AI passwords or API keys. Please avoid including personal details in your doubt.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase and Google Sign-In for accounts and data storage.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced embed) when a project video is shown. YouTube may collect data when you play a video.
  • Shopping links (for example Amazon or Flipkart) open only when you tap them and are governed by those sites.
  • Code libraries from public CDNs on some pages, such as the Code Runner.

8. Security

  • The site is served over HTTPS.
  • Sign-in uses Google, so we do not hold passwords.
  • Firestore security rules limit each student to their own records; administrator tools are limited to approved administrator accounts.
  • Payment references can be used once only.

No website can promise perfect security, so please keep your Google account protected.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep your account, project and review data while your account is active. Payment records are kept as long as needed for verification, support and accounting. You can ask us to delete your account data; some payment records may need to be kept for legal or accounting reasons.

10. Your choices

  • Update your name and avatar from your profile.
  • Delete your reviews and selected projects yourself.
  • Change cookie choices at any time.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data, or to explain what we hold about you.

11. Students under 18

Many diploma students are teenagers. If you are under 18, please use LONY with the knowledge of a parent or guardian, and avoid sharing personal details in reviews or in questions to AI services.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

For questions or data requests, contact LONY Projects through the contact options on the home page.
